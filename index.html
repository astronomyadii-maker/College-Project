Welcome

Welcome to the official website of Indira Gandhi Engineering College Sagar , MP. We are committed to providing quality technical education and shaping future engineers through modern facilities, experienced faculty, and a strong focus on practical learning.

Facilities

Library

Well-stocked central library with thousands of books, journals, and e-resources.

Hostel

Separate hostels for boys and girls with 24x7 security and Wi-Fi facility.

Computer Labs

Modern computer labs with high-speed internet for practical sessions.

Sports Ground

Facilities for cricket, football, volleyball, and indoor games.

Transport

College buses covering major routes across the city.

Canteen

Hygienic and affordable food available for students and staff.

Departments

Civil Engineering

Focuses on construction, structural design, and infrastructure development.

Mechanical Engineering

Covers manufacturing, thermodynamics, and machine design.

Electrical Engineering

Deals with power systems, circuits, and electrical machines.

Information Technology & Engineering

Covers programming, data structures, AI, and software development.

Electronics & Communication

Focuses on communication systems, embedded systems, and circuits.

About Us

Indira Gandhi Engineering College Sagar, MP has been a leading name in technical education for over three decades. Our mission is to nurture talent through quality teaching, industry exposure, and hands-on training, preparing students to excel in their professional careers.
